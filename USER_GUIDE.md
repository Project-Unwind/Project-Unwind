## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Create an account** using your email address.
2. **Verify your email** with the one-time code (OTP) sent to your inbox.
3. **Complete onboarding** — set up your profile and preferences.
4. **Log in** anytime with your email and password, or use "Remember Me" to stay signed in on trusted devices.

Forgot your password? Use **Forgot Password** on the login screen to receive a secure reset link.

<br/>

## 🏠 Your Dashboard

Your dashboard is the home base for your wellness journey. From here you can:

- See a summary of your recent mood, activity, and journal entries
- View your current wellness streaks
- Jump directly into an assessment, journal entry, or wellness task
- See personalized recommendations based on your recent activity

<br/>

## 🧠 DASS-21 Self-Assessment

The DASS-21 is a structured 21-question self-assessment covering **depression, anxiety, and stress**.

**How to take it:**

1. Go to the **Assessment** section from your dashboard.
2. Answer all 21 questions honestly, based on how you've felt recently.
3. Submit to see your results — a separate score and severity level for each of the three areas.
4. Review your **history** to see how your scores have changed over time.
5. Download a **PDF report** of your results to keep or share with a professional, if you choose.

> Your DASS-21 results are a reflection tool, not a diagnosis. If your results concern you, consider speaking with a qualified mental-health professional.

## 📔 Private Journal

Your journal is a protected space for personal reflection.

**Setting up journal security:**

- The first time you open your journal, you'll set a dedicated **Journal PIN** — separate from your account password.
- If you forget your PIN, you can recover access via an OTP sent to your verified email.
- You can unlock your journal for a temporary session so you don't need to re-enter your PIN for every entry during that session.

**Writing entries:**

- Start from a blank entry or choose a **writing prompt** for inspiration.
- Tag entries with emotions, activities, and custom tags to organize them.
- Save as a **draft** or mark an entry as **complete**.
- Mark meaningful entries as **favorites** for quick access later.
- Attach relevant files or images to an entry, if needed.
- Hide entry previews on the journal list for extra privacy on shared screens.

**Managing entries:**

- Archive entries you want to keep but don't need to see regularly.
- Delete entries you no longer want — deleted entries go to a recoverable state first, with the option to permanently delete them later.

- Log daily wellness activities such as sleep, hydration, and self-care tasks.
- Track habit-building progress over time.
- View your engagement streaks and recent activity from your dashboard.

## 💬 Community

Unwind's community spaces let you connect with others at your own comfort level.

**Posts & interactions:**

- Share posts with text, images, or videos.
- Like, comment, and share posts from others.
- Like individual comments to show support.

**Rooms:**

- Join **public rooms** for open discussion around shared topics.
- Join or create **private rooms** using an invite for more focused, trusted conversations.

**Identity & safety:**

- Choose your visibility preferences to control how your identity appears in community spaces.
- Report any post, comment, or user that violates community guidelines — reports are reviewed by the moderation team.

<br/>

## ✉️ Direct Messages

- Send one-to-one messages to other users in real time.
- See **typing indicators**, **online presence**, and **read receipts**.
- **Reply** to specific messages or **edit** a message you've sent.
- Unread message counts appear so you never lose track of a conversation.

<br/>

## 🔔 Notifications

- Open your **Notification Center** to see recent account and community activity.
- Filter notifications by type.
- Mark individual notifications as read, or use bulk actions to clear multiple at once.

<br/>

## ⚙️ Account & Privacy Settings

From your account settings, you can:

- Update your profile information and preferences
- Change your password
- Log out from a single device or **all devices** at once
- Review and manage your journal PIN security
- Adjust your identity visibility for community features

<br/>

## 🎨 Themes & Accessibility

- Switch between **light** and **dark** themes based on your preference.
- Motion effects respect your device's reduced-motion settings automatically.
- The interface is designed to work smoothly across desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

<details>
<summary><strong>Is my journal really private?</strong></summary>
<br/>
Yes. Your journal is protected by a separate PIN in addition to your account login, adding an extra layer of privacy beyond your regular account security.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Does Unwind diagnose mental health conditions?</strong></summary>
<br/>
No. Unwind's assessments and tools are designed for self-reflection and awareness. They are not a medical diagnosis and should not replace professional care.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can other users see my journal entries?</strong></summary>
<br/>
No. Journal entries are private to your account and protected by your journal PIN. They are separate from community posts, which you choose to share publicly or within rooms.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What happens if I delete a journal entry by mistake?</strong></summary>
<br/>
Deleted entries are moved to a recoverable state first rather than being permanently removed immediately, giving you a chance to restore them.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Who can see my identity in community spaces?</strong></summary>
<br/>
You control your visibility preferences, which determine how your identity appears in posts, comments, and rooms.
</details>
